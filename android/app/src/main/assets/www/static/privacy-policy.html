Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Version 1.0.1

Welcome to OnTracker ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Reyansh. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our habit tracking and student focus application handles your information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

OnTrack is designed with a Privacy-First & Offline-First architecture:

  • Account Information: When you create an account or sign in with Google Firebase, we collect your email address and an encrypted user identifier to isolate your personal habit data from other users.
  • Habits & Routine Data: Names of habits, categories, target values, daily check-in timestamps, streak history, and RPG level progress are stored locally on your device storage and backed up securely if signed into Firebase.
  • Focus & Display Preferences: Theme modes (Light, Dark, Paper), tactile feedback settings, and study reminder preferences are stored locally in your device's private cache (localStorage).
  • Guest Mode: You can use OnTrack completely as a Guest without creating an account. In Guest mode, all your data stays strictly on your device.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver scheduled habit cues, streak milestone reminders, and timer alerts that you choose to enable. We never send advertising or spam notifications.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide subtle tactile haptic confirmation when completing a habit or when a study timer completes.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify network connectivity when syncing your account with Firebase and loading your cloud habits.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide, personalize, and improve your habit tracking and focus experience:

  • Calculating habit strength formulas (based on the Loop Habit Tracker algorithm).
  • Generating personalized recovery protocols and Pomodoro rest intervals.
  • Preventing data bleed between multiple user accounts on the same device.

4. Third-Party Services

OnTrack integrates with trusted third-party developer platforms:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: To handle secure password encryption and user sign-in. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal habit records, email address, or usage analytics to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • You can clear your local data anytime directly within the App's Settings or your Android device's App Storage settings.
  • If you wish to delete your Firebase account and cloud habit records, you can tap "Delete Account & Data" in Settings or email us at support@ontrack.app.

6. Children's Privacy

OnTrack does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for immediate deletion.
